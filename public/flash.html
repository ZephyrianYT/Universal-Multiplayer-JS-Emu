🎞️ Load a .SWF

Play a classic Flash game from a .swf file on your computer, like putting a cartridge in. It runs in this tab with Ruffle, a Flash Player emulator. The file stays on your computer (nothing is uploaded) and the game gets no internet access. Saves are kept in this browser.

Most older Flash games (ActionScript 1/2) work. Newer ActionScript 3 games may run with glitches or not at all. Games that need their own website or an online server won't work.
