Off Site Games

These games run on other websites, not on this hub: they open in a new tab, and lobbies, netplay, saves and achievements here don't apply. Retro Hub doesn't host or check them; each site has its own rules and ads.

                    RetroAchievements

                    Shows each game's achievements and your progress while you play, and tells you (and the lobby, if you like) when one unlocks. Your Web API key is on retroachievements.org/settings under Authentication (it can only read, never play as you). Both stay in this browser; the hub passes them to RetroAchievements and keeps nothing.

                    Offsite · retroachievements.orgNo account yet? Sign up on RetroAchievements ↗ Free. Your account lives there, not on this hub.

                    Achievements unlock inside an emulator with RetroAchievements built in, such as the hub's native emulators (PCSX2, Dolphin, DuckStation, PPSSPP, melonDS…) signed in to your account. Browser games (EmulatorJS) can show the list and your progress, but can't unlock.
